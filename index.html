Welcome to the Digital Heritage Repository

Welcome to the digital curation repository. This curated site features five primary digital artifacts representing essential indigenous, archival, and political histories of South Africa Sourced directly from key archival institutions--including the National Archives and Records Service of South Africa, Univerity of Pretoria Museum, The British Museum, and Robben Island Archives--each item is presented with Dublin Core metadata and contextual research.
